# Taiwanese academy map

Created with built-in imagegen, using the project's daytime campus only as the layout reference. PNG original and lossless WebP are stored here as `campus-taiwan.png` and `campus-taiwan.webp`. The generated image is an original fictional campus, with architectural inspiration rather than a reproduction of a real campus or school endorsement.

Design references: [NTU campus architecture](https://ntuweb.cloud.ntu.edu.tw/12scence/03.htm), [NTHU architecture timeline](https://www.lib.nthu.edu.tw/events/2022/architecture/timeline.html), [NCKU red-brick arcades](https://exptainan.liberal.ncku.edu.tw/index.php?id=3546&index=5&lang=cht&option=module&task=showlist).

Final prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept. Edit target: supplied daytime game campus. Create the FINAL original Taiwanese university pharmacy academy map in the same polished warm illustrated 3D storybook game style, landscape 1536x1024. Keep the exact five building centers, entrance positions, central fountain/plaza and radial connecting pedestrian paths as the input so interactive hit areas stay aligned: upper-left center (20%,27%) entrance (23%,37%); upper-center (51%,21%) entrance (50%,32%); upper-right (83%,31%) entrance (81%,41%); lower-right (81%,66%) entrance (82%,76%); lower-left (22%,60%) entrance (24%,69%). Replace ALL European fairy-tale castles, pointed towers, domes, medieval bridges and floating fantasy cliffs with a believable lush sunny Taiwan university campus, slightly whimsical diorama but recognizable Taiwan academic architecture. Every building belongs to ONLY ONE selected university-style family, with distinct visual form: 1 upper-left PHARMACEUTICS, National Taiwan University style, rich muted terracotta red-brick facade, cream stone-trim arched windows and arched shaded corridor, dark tiled pitched roof, symmetrical academic entrance and palm trees. 2 upper-center PHARMACOLOGY, National Tsing Hua University style, clean low-rise horizontal ivory-white modernist research block, rectangular windows, deep shade fins, planted courtyards and lush green trees, no red brick. 3 upper-right HERBAL, National Cheng Kung University style, long heritage red-brick low-rise building with continuous rounded Roman arcades and gray-tile roof next to a large banyan tree, an attached small contemporary medicinal herb greenhouse, no gothic forms. 4 lower-right BATTLE, National Tsing Hua University style, contemporary white research and learning hall in two broad rectangular wings joined by a glass skywalk, refined mint/blue glass, open ground-level arcade, no fortress towers. 5 lower-left WORKSHOP, National Cheng Kung University style, smaller warm red-brick heritage workshop with gray pitched roof, wooden shutters, deep veranda of rounded arches, herb planters and outdoor craft tables. Preserve exact building placement but change architecture substantially. Shared surroundings: broad warm pale paving, palm and banyan canopies, fern beds, vivid but gentle grass, blue sky, luminous clear early-morning sunlight, small turquoise shallow central fountain, landscaped real ground not cliffs. Color palette cream, fresh mint, sky blue, brick red accents. Quiet inviting learning haven. No people, no characters, no text, letters, logos, school crests, watermarks or signs. Do not reproduce any exact real campus plan or landmark, only original designs informed by these distinct architectural vocabularies.



## Deployment encoding
The PNG generation masters remain unchanged locally. For mobile loading, the deployed WebP files were subsequently re-encoded at quality 90 at the same dimensions with lossless alpha. Initial lossless-conversion hashes above refer to the pre-optimization files; current runtime sizes and SHA-256 values are in `runtime-assets.json`.
